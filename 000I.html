Definition. Airfoil [000I]

  • My forest

An airfoil is any surface that provides aerodynamic forces when it interacts with a moving stream of air.

  • Leading edge: the part of the airfoil which meets the airflow first.
  • Upwash: the deflection of the oncoming airstream upward and over the wing.
  • Trailing edge: the portion of the airfoil where the airflow over the upper surface rejoins the lower surface airflow.
  • Downwash: the downward deflection of the airstream as it passes over and under the wing and past the trailing edge.
