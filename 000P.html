Definition. Planform [000P]

  • PPL

Planform refers to the shape of the airplane’s wing.

  • Elliptical wing

    • Pros

      • Ideal for flight at low speeds because it provides minimal drag for a given aspect ratio.

    • Cons

      • Difficult to construct.
      • Bad stall characteristics.

  • Rectangular wing

    • Pros

      • Tendency to stall first at the wing root which provides adequate stall warning and aileron effectiveness.

    • Cons

      • Not as efficient as elliptical wing.

  • Tapered wing

    • Pros

      • Tapering provides a decrease in drag and increase in lift which is most effective at high speeds.

    • Cons

      • A highly tapered wing has a tendency to stall slightly inboard the wingtip.

  • Sweptback wing and delta wing

    • Pros

      • Allows the airplane to fly at a higher speed without reaching the critial Mach number, the speed at which the wing experiences supersonic airflow.

    • Cons

      • A significant part of the air velocity is flowing spanwise, not contributing to lift. This raises the stall speed and also causes the wingtips to stall first.
