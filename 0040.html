Definition. Lifting property [0040]

Let and be two morphisms in a category . We say that has the left lifting property (respectively, has the right lifting property) if whenever there is a commutative square below, there exists a morphism filling the square, i.e. making the two triangles commute.

We say that has the orthogonal left lifting property if it has the left lifting property and for all commutative squares, the filling is unique.
