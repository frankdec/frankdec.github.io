Definition. Angle of attack [000J]

  • PPL
  • Relative wind: the airflow which is parallel to and opposite the flight path of the airplane.
  • Chord line: a straight line passing through the leading edge and the trailing edge.
  • Camber: the characteristic curve of the airfoil’s upper and lower surfaces.

The angle of attack (AoA) of an airfoil is the angle between the chord line of the airfoil and the direction of the relative wind.

A negative AoA indicates that the chord line of the airfoil is below the direction of the relative wind, while a positive AoA indicates the opposite.
