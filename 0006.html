Definition. Isomorphism [0006]

  • Model Theory

An embedding is an isomorphism if it is surjective, or equivalently if there is an inverse homomorphism .

Remark. [#]

    A bijective homomorphism is not necessarily an isomorphism because it may not reflect relations, i.e., the inverse may not be a homomorphism.
